Black Baba

Moldovian desert, one of the bests

The way to make the best black old woman ever.

"Baba Neagră" is a traditional Moldovan dish that housewives in northern Moldova have been preparing for years; it is considered the "queen" of the five traditional Moldovan dishes—essential for any festive meal—and is something guests eagerly look forward to savoring.

Ingredients

  • 10 eggs
  • 500 ml milk
  • 250 ml sour milk (or kefir)
  • 250–300 g sugar (depending on your preferred sweetness)
  • 180 ml oil
  • 2 cups flour (using a 250 ml cup level-filled and not packed down)
  • 1½ tablespoons baking soda (30 g by weight)
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 25 ml vodka
  1. STEP 1 — EGGS

    2. Place the eggs and sugar in a deep bowl and whisk until the sugar dissolves (2–3 minutes).

  2. STEP 2 — MIXING

    3. Add the flour and vanilla sugar, and whisk the mixture to ensure there are no lumps. Add the oil and stir; then, while continuing to mix, gradually incorporate the milk and *chișleag* (fermented milk). Add the baking soda and mix thoroughly to distribute it evenly, followed by the vodka. That is the order in which I added all the ingredients.

  3. STEP 3 — THE CAULDRON

    4. Grease the cauldron with butter or margarine and dust it with breadcrumbs or flour.

  4. STEP 4 — POT

    5. We pour the mixture into the cauldron.

  5. STEP 5 — ALUMINIUM

    6. As you can see, I’ve wrapped the cauldron in aluminum foil. I do this so the *babuță* doesn’t burn and turns out looking good, not just tasting delicious.

  6. STEP 6 — COOK

    7. Bake the *babă* for 2 hours at 180–200°C (depending on how your oven bakes), then lower the temperature to 120–150°C and bake for another hour. I’d like to add a note: if you notice that the *babă* isn't dark enough in color after 3 hours of baking, let it bake for another hour, as ovens vary. Tested.

  7. STEP 7 - ENJOY

    8. We take care not to open the oven while the *baba* is baking. After three hours of baking, we turn off the heat and let the *baba* sit in the oven for another hour, after which we can remove it.
